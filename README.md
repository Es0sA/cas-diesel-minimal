# CAS Energy: Bulk Diesel Marketplace & Escrow Logistics

An enterprise downstream petroleum trading platform connecting corporate buyers, licensed marketers, and calibrated fleet drivers across Nigeria.

## Core Features

- **Live Depot Spot Ticker:** Real-time certified AGO pricing across Apapa, Ijegun Egba, Warri, Oghara, Port Harcourt, and Calabar.
- **The Geofence Escrow Terminal:** Two-sided escrow protection model where funds are locked upfront, transit is non-cancellable once loaded, and discharge sign-off is geofenced within 100 meters of the buyer gate.
- **Buyer Facility Registration:** Mandatory GPS pin drop with truck height and axle clearance parameters.
- **Marketer Pricing Desk:** Real-time spot price broadcasting and calibrated fleet allocation.
- **Driver Mobile Cockpit:** One-tap turn-by-turn navigation, waypoint seals, and delivery milestone updates.
- **Full Legal & Regulatory Disclosures:** Terms of service, NDPR privacy policy, quality dispute and refund protocols, and essential cookie governance.
